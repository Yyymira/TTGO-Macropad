What goes to each GPIO of the TTGO T-Display for the 10 buttons (6 Cherry MX, the board's 2 built-in buttons and each encoder's push switch) and the rotation of the 2 encoders. Everything is soldered straight to the switch pins, against the same GND bus.

Parts list

  • 6× Cherry MX switches (Brown), plate mount, snapped into the case lid.
  • 2× EC11 rotary encoders, preferably with a push switch (3 rotation pins: A – C – B, with C to GND; 2 switch pins). If yours has 3 pins (no switch), don't wire GPIO15 or GPIO2: everything else works the same and buttons 9 and 10 just aren't used.
  • Thin wire to solder straight to the pins (no PCB).

Notes

  • Every switch goes to GND using the ESP32's internal pull-up — no resistors or diodes (each key has its own GPIO, there's no matrix).
  • The encoders use the same internal pull-up through ESP32Encoder.
  • GPIO0 is also the boot button: if you hold it while plugging in USB, the board enters flashing mode instead of booting normally.
  • GPIO12, GPIO15 and GPIO2 are also read at boot, but the encoder and the switches only pull them to GND: when released they change nothing. Don't hold encoder 2 pressed while flashing.
